# Support

## Getting help

- **Using a template:** start with the [docs site](https://leanmeanclean.dev/), then that repository's README. They're long because they answer the questions people actually ask.
- **Still stuck, or just curious?** Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/LeanMeanCleanApps/discussions). Someone else probably wanted to know too.
- **Found a bug or have an idea?** Open an issue in the repository it's about. A clear title and a way to reproduce it will get you a faster answer than a long story.
- **Something got in your way?** Report an [accessibility barrier](https://github.com/LeanMeanCleanApps/leanmeancleanapps.github.io/issues/new?template=accessibility_barrier.yml). The [accessibility statement](ACCESSIBILITY.md) says what happens next.
- **Found a security vulnerability?** Don't open an issue. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else, or something you'd rather not say in public?** Email [hello@leanmeanclean.dev](mailto:hello@leanmeanclean.dev).

These are [Lean Mean Clean Apps](https://leanmeanclean.dev/) projects, looked after in spare time by a team that is currently one person with a day job and a coffee habit, so replies are best effort. They do arrive.
