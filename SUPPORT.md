# Support

## Getting help

- **Using a template:** start with that repository's README. It's long because it answers the questions people actually ask.
- **Found a bug or have an idea?** Open an issue in the repository it's about. A clear title and a way to reproduce it will get you a faster answer than a long story.
- **Found a security vulnerability?** Don't open an issue. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

These are personal open source projects, maintained in spare time by one person with a day job and a coffee habit, so replies are best effort. They do arrive.
